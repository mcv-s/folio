# Widgets & Addons

## What are widgets? 

Widgets are the little modules that can display information or be interacted with on your Folio new tab.

Want a timer or an RSS feed on your new tab page? That's a widget.

Folio contains a variety of widgets designed to help you be more productive and make your life simpler: but they are all completely optional and do not need to be used or enabled. 

## How do I anchor my widgets?

Drag a widget around and then hold A to open the Radial Anchor Menu. 

![Preview](/Images/WidgetAnchoring.png)

This menu allows you to choose what edge of the screen your widget is anchored. 




## What are addons/integrations?

Currently, most of the addons come in the form of widgets to help you display the information you need. Request an addon on [our github.](https://github.com/mcv-s/folio)
